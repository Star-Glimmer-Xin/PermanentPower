# PermanentPower

[简体中文](README.md) | **English**

A Slay the Spire 2 mod: Power cards stay in effect permanently.

## Main Features

- A Power card played is recorded and removed from your deck. You gain its effect at the start of every combat
- Cards can be enabled or disabled globally, for modded cards as a group, or individually

## Installation

Requirements:

- Slay the Spire 2 `0.111.0`
- [RitsuLib](https://github.com/BAKAOLC/STS2-RitsuLib) `0.5.20` or newer

Drop `PermanentPower.dll` and `PermanentPower.json` into `mods/PermanentPower/` inside your game folder:

```
Slay the Spire 2/
  mods/
    PermanentPower/
      PermanentPower.dll
      PermanentPower.json
```

The first launch asks whether to enable mods; say yes and restart once.
